Authenticity Verdict — Most Sellers Are Pakistani-Made, Arabic-Inspired (click to expand)

Key Finding
The vast majority of Pakistani abaya sellers sell Pakistani-made products with Arabic-inspired names and styling. Genuine Gulf/Arabic abayas are rare.
Irani Abaya ≠ Arabic
Persian chador style. NOT Gulf Arabic. Commonly mislabelled — even by buyers.
Iraqi Abaya ≠ Arabic
Also Persian-influenced. NOT Khaleeji/Gulf. A common misconception in PK market.
True Arabic = Khaleeji / Gulf
Sourced from Saudi Arabia, UAE, or Kuwait. Open-front silhouette, premium fabrics, Gulf manufacturing.

6-Step Launch Playbook

1
Build Instagram First — 30 Days Before Buying Any Stock
Post daily Reels for 30 days before purchasing inventory. Validate demand, build an audience, and see which styles get engagement. Don't risk capital before you have social proof and market validation.
2
Source from Deira / Naif Souq Dubai — Al Mandoos Trading LLC
Deira and Naif Souq in Dubai are the primary wholesale hubs for authentic Gulf abayas. Al Mandoos Trading LLC is a known supplier. Build a relationship with a reliable exporter to secure consistent authentic supply and provenance documentation.
3
Set Up WhatsApp Business Properly Before Launch
Configure a product catalog, quick replies for common questions, and a broadcast list from Day 1. WhatsApp is the real checkout — treat it like your storefront. Rs.300–500 advance is standard to filter serious buyers and reduce fake COD.
4
Join All 3 Facebook Groups — Study for 2 Weeks Before Posting
Join the Online Gown/Burqa Store, Arabic Style Dubai Abaya, and Queen Abaya Collection groups. Observe buying patterns, price resistance, and top-selling styles for two full weeks. Then engage with value before ever promoting anything.
5
Position Explicitly as Authentic Arabic — Not Irani
Clearly communicate that your products are Gulf/Khaleeji (Saudi, UAE) — not Irani or Iraqi. This distinction matters to informed buyers and commands a significant premium. Use terms like "Khaleeji," "Gulf-style," "Saudi Nidah," and "UAE import" deliberately and consistently.
6
Always Sell Bundles — Full Sets Increase Order Value 40–60%
Bundle abaya + niqab + gloves into a complete modest set. Bundles reduce buyer hesitation, increase average order value by 40–60%, and differentiate you from single-item sellers. Offer a modest discount vs. individual prices to drive conversion while maintaining healthy margins.
